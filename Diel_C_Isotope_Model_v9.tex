\subsection{Tri-Objective Optimisation · T-Dependent ER ·
Isotope-Enabled
PHREEQC}\label{tri-objective-optimisation-t-dependent-er-isotope-enabled-phreeqc}

This model simultaneously fits \textbf{three observational targets}:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{δ¹³C-DIC} --- carbon isotope composition of dissolved
  inorganic carbon
\item
  \textbf{GPP} --- gross primary productivity (PAR × Michaelis--Menten ×
  Q₁₀)
\item
  \textbf{ER} --- ecosystem respiration (Q₁₀ temperature dependence)
\end{enumerate}

with bounded differential evolution and Bayesian penalty priors on all 8
calibrated parameters.

\subsection{Step 8 --- Forward model and tri-objective
function}\label{step-8-forward-model-and-tri-objective-function}

\textbf{GPP} = GPP\_max × PAR/(I\_k + PAR) × Q₁₀,GPP\^{}((T−T\_ref)/10)

\textbf{ER} = ER\_base × Q₁₀,ER\^{}((T−T\_ref)/10) --- now
temperature-dependent, not constant

\textbf{Tri-objective cost}:
\[J = \mathrm{RMSE}(\delta^{13}C) + 0.05 \cdot \mathrm{RMSE}(\mathrm{GPP}) + 1.0 \cdot \mathrm{RMSE}(\mathrm{ER}) + \sum_k \lambda_k \left(\frac{\theta_k - \mu_k}{\sigma_k}\right)^2\]

The weight on ER (1.0) reflects that its observed range
(\textasciitilde1.1 mg/L/h) is comparable to the δ¹³C range
(\textasciitilde1.4 ‰), so both terms contribute equally to the cost.
GPP is down-weighted (0.05) because its range (\textasciitilde30 mg/L/h)
is much larger.

\subsubsection{Key result}\label{key-result}

The Q₁₀ temperature dependence captures the diel ER variation
(\textasciitilde1.1 mg O₂/L/h range) driven by the \textasciitilde3.7 °C
temperature swing. With a constant ER (v8), the model could not
reproduce the observed nighttime-low / afternoon-high ER pattern.
